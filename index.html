Privacy Policy – ShiftLive Ride Chat

Last Updated: February 2026

This Extension is intended exclusively for authorized ShiftLive business users operating under a contractual agreement with Shift Live Ltd.

1. General

Shift Live Ltd. ("Company", "We", "Us") provides the Chrome extension "ShiftLive Ride Chat" (the “Extension”). The Extension enables authorized users to send chat messages to selected rides within the ShiftLive system.

2. Data Access

The Extension may temporarily access:

  • Authorization header (access token)
  • Customer ID
  • Ride identifiers (Ride IDs)

3. Purpose of Processing

  • Authenticating API requests
  • Identifying the relevant customer account
  • Sending ride chat messages

4. Data Storage & Retention

No personal data is stored persistently. All session data is processed in memory and automatically discarded when the session ends.

5. Data Sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. All communication occurs directly with ShiftLive servers over HTTPS.

6. No Tracking

The Extension does not track browsing activity or collect analytics.

7. Security

All communications are encrypted via HTTPS. No third-party tracking tools are included.

8. Legal Basis (GDPR)

Processing is performed under GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and/or 6(1)(f), where applicable.
