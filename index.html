Privacy Policy – ShiftLive Ride Chat

Last Updated: February 2026

This Extension is intended exclusively for authorized ShiftLive business users operating under a contractual agreement with Shift Live Ltd.

1. General

Shift Live Ltd. ("Company", "We", "Us") provides the Chrome extension "ShiftLive Ride Chat" (the “Extension”). The Extension enables authorized ShiftLive users to send chat messages to selected rides within the ShiftLive system.

2. Data Access

During active use within ShiftLive domains, the Extension may temporarily access:

  • Authorization header (access token)
  • Customer ID
  • Ride identifiers (Ride IDs)

This data is accessed strictly to enable secure communication with ShiftLive servers.

3. Purpose of Processing

  • Authenticating API requests
  • Identifying the relevant customer account
  • Sending ride chat messages

No analytics, profiling, or tracking is performed.

4. Data Storage & Retention

The Extension does not store personal data persistently. All session data is processed transiently in memory and automatically discarded when:

  • The extension popup is closed
  • The browser tab is closed
  • The browser session ends

No external storage systems are used.

5. Data Sharing

The Extension does not sell, rent, transfer, or share data with any third parties. All communication occurs directly between the user's browser and ShiftLive servers over secure HTTPS connections.

6. No Tracking

The Extension does not track browsing activity outside ShiftLive-related domains and does not collect analytics or advertising identifiers.

7. Security

All communications are encrypted via HTTPS. No third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or tracking libraries are included in the Extension.

8. Legal Basis (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), processing is performed under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract) and/or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests), where applicable.

9. Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
