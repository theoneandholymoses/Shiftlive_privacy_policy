Privacy Policy – ShiftLive Ride Chat

Last Updated: February 2026

1. General

Shift Live Ltd. ("Company", "We", "Us", "Our") provides a Chrome browser extension named "ShiftLive Ride Chat" (the “Extension”).

The Extension is designed exclusively for authorized users of the ShiftLive platform and enables sending chat messages to selected rides within the ShiftLive system.

2. Intended Users

The Extension is intended solely for authorized ShiftLive customers and users operating under a valid contractual agreement with Shift Live Ltd.

The Extension is not intended for public consumer use.

3. Data Accessed by the Extension

During active use within ShiftLive web domains, the Extension may temporarily access:

  • Authorization header (access token)
  • Customer ID
  • Ride identifiers (Ride IDs)

This information is accessed strictly to enable ride chat functionality within the ShiftLive platform.

4. Purpose of Processing

The accessed session data is processed solely for:

  • Authenticating API requests to ShiftLive servers
  • Identifying the relevant customer account
  • Sending chat messages to selected rides

No additional profiling, analytics, tracking, or monitoring is performed.

5. Data Storage & Retention

The Extension does not store personal data persistently.

All accessed session data is processed transiently in memory during the active browser session. Such data is automatically discarded when:

  • The extension popup is closed
  • The relevant browser tab is closed
  • The browser session ends

No external databases, cloud storage services, or third-party storage providers are used.

6. Data Sharing

The Extension does not sell, rent, transfer, or share collected data with any third parties.

All communication occurs directly between the user’s browser and ShiftLive servers over secure HTTPS connections.

7. No Tracking

The Extension does not track browsing activity outside ShiftLive-related domains.

It does not collect analytics data, behavioral data, or advertising identifiers.

8. Security Measures

All communications are performed over encrypted HTTPS connections.

The Extension does not transmit data to any external services other than ShiftLive servers. No third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or tracking libraries are included.

9. Legal Basis (GDPR)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), data processing is performed under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (processing necessary for the performance of a contract) and/or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests), as applicable.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published at this URL with a revised “Last Updated” date.
