Privacy Policy – ShiftLive Ride Chat

Last Updated: February 2026

This Chrome Extension is intended exclusively for authorized business users of the ShiftLive platform operating under a valid contractual agreement with Shift Live Ltd.

1. General

Shift Live Ltd. ("Shift", "Company", "We", "Us", "Our") provides the Chrome browser extension "ShiftLive Ride Chat" (the “Extension”). The Extension forms part of the ShiftLive transportation management system and enables authorized users to send chat messages to rides within the ShiftLive platform.

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the Extension. It does not apply to third-party websites, applications, or services that may be linked to or integrated with ShiftLive.

3. Information Accessed by the Extension

During active use within ShiftLive web domains, the Extension may temporarily access:

  • Authorization header (access token)
  • Customer identifier
  • Ride identifiers (Ride IDs)

The Extension does not independently collect personal data beyond what is required to enable ride chat functionality.

4. Purpose of Processing

The accessed information is processed strictly for:

  • Authenticating API requests to ShiftLive servers
  • Associating messages with the correct customer account
  • Enabling chat communication related to rides

No profiling, analytics, advertising, or behavioral tracking is performed.

5. Data Storage & Retention

The Extension does not store personal data persistently. All accessed session data is processed transiently in memory and automatically discarded when:

  • The extension popup is closed
  • The browser tab is closed
  • The browser session ends

No external databases or third-party storage providers are used by the Extension.

6. Disclosure to Third Parties

The Extension does not sell, rent, or transfer personal data to third parties.

Information is transmitted solely to ShiftLive servers as part of providing the Service. We may disclose information if required by applicable law, regulation, or lawful order.

7. Security Safeguards

All communication between the Extension and ShiftLive servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.

Shift Live Ltd. operates in accordance with internationally recognized information security standards, including ISO 27001, ISO 27017, ISO 27018, and ISO 27032. Appropriate organizational and technical safeguards are implemented to protect data against unauthorized access or misuse.

8. Retention Period

Since the Extension does not retain data independently, no long-term retention occurs within the Extension environment. Data processed through the ShiftLive platform is retained in accordance with the Company’s general data retention policies.

9. Rights of Data Subjects (EEA)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you may have rights under the GDPR, including rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection, subject to applicable law. Requests may be submitted to: info@shiftlive.net

10. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to amend this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
